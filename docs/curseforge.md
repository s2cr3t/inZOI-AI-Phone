## inZOI AI Phone（HouseholdBoard_I6IXIO）

**Summary**: A smartphone for your Zois. Family and friends text back with AI. 家人朋友会真的给你回消息。

### Description

> ⚠️ **This mod needs AI Phone Service to reply.** Download the installer from GitHub:
> **https://github.com/s2cr3t/inZOI-AI-Phone/releases/latest**
> 这个 mod 需要配合「AI 手机服务」才能回消息。请从上面的 GitHub 地址下载安装包。

**English**

Your Zoi gets a real smartphone. Text your family and friends, and they answer in character, based on personality, relationship and history, and remember what you talked about. When they agree to something in chat, it happens in the game.

- **AI texting**: every Zoi replies in their own voice
- **They text first**: after work, when they get home, when you are out late or not doing well
- **Moments**: Zois post updates, like and comment on each other's posts, and reply to your comments
- **Group chats**: a whole household or a group of friends in one chat
- **They act on it**: order you takeout, buy a car, change moods and preferences, run in-game interactions
- **A real phone**: app store, wallpapers, dark mode, small apps such as News
- AI Phone is free. Pick an AI provider such as DeepSeek in the phone's AI page and add your own API key; the provider bills your own account.

**How to install**

1. Install this mod and **inZOI AI Phone - Game Link** (both are required).
2. Download and run **inZOI-AI-Phone-Setup.exe** from the GitHub link above. It sets up the AI service on your PC.
3. Start inZOI and open the phone. If the service is missing, the phone shows a setup guide with a copy-link button.

Room Lights is optional.

**简体中文**

给你的 Zoi 一部真正的智能手机。给家人朋友发消息，他们会按自己的性格、关系和经历回复你，记得你们聊过的事。聊天里答应的事，游戏里真的会去做。

- **AI 短信**：每个角色用自己的口吻回复
- **主动来信**：下班、到家、深夜没回家、状态不好的时候，家人朋友会主动发消息
- **朋友圈**：角色会发动态、互相点赞评论，也会回复你的评论
- **群聊**：一家人或一群朋友在同一个群里聊天
- **真的会去做**：帮你点外卖、买车、改变心情和喜好、做游戏里的互动
- **完整的手机**：应用商店、壁纸、深色模式，新闻等小应用
- AI 手机本身免费。在手机的 AI 页面选择 AI 服务商（如 DeepSeek），填入你自己的 API 密钥，调用费用由你自己的账号承担。

**安装方法**

1. 安装这个 mod 和 **inZOI AI Phone - Game Link**，两个都必须装。
2. 从上面的 GitHub 地址下载 **inZOI-AI-Phone-Setup.exe** 并运行，它会在电脑上装好 AI 手机服务。
3. 启动 inZOI，打开手机。如果服务没装，手机里会显示安装引导，并提供复制下载地址的按钮。

Room Lights（自动开关灯）可装可不装。

## inZOI AI Phone - Game Link（HouseholdJournal_N4CZ4Z）

**Summary**: Required by inZOI AI Phone. Lets the phone read your household and carry out what Zois agree to. inZOI AI 手机的必装组件。

### Description

**English**

Game-side helper for **inZOI AI Phone**. It lets the phone know who lives in your household and where everyone is, and carries out what Zois agree to in chat, such as ordering takeout, buying a car or running an in-game interaction. It does nothing on its own: install it together with inZOI AI Phone and AI Phone Service.

AI Phone Service: https://github.com/s2cr3t/inZOI-AI-Phone/releases/latest

**简体中文**

**inZOI AI 手机**的游戏端组件。它让手机知道家里有谁、大家在哪，并把聊天里答应的事（点外卖、买车、游戏里的互动）在游戏里执行。单独安装没有作用，需要和 inZOI AI 手机、AI 手机服务一起使用。

AI 手机服务下载：https://github.com/s2cr3t/inZOI-AI-Phone/releases/latest

## Room Lights（RoomLights_N4CZ4Z）

**Summary**: Lights turn on in rooms where someone is home and turn off in empty rooms. 有人开灯，没人关灯。

### Description

**English**

Lights follow your Zois around the house: a room lights up when someone walks in and goes dark when everyone has left. Works on its own, no other mod or service needed.

**简体中文**

灯跟着 Zoi 走：有人进屋就开灯，人都走了就关灯。可以单独使用，不需要其他 mod 或服务。

From the inZOI AI Phone project: https://github.com/s2cr3t/inZOI-AI-Phone
